praying images

The school believes in God as the source of wisdom, guidance,
and strength for every learner and teacher. It encourages prayer
as a daily practice to build discipline, hope,
and a strong moral character.
Through faith in God, the school promotes unity, respect, and a
peaceful learning environment for everyone.

refectory images

The student refectory is a shared lunching space designed to provide a calm, clean,
and welcoming environment where students can enjoy their meals.
It promotes healthy eating habits, respect, and community spirit among learners.
Students are expected to maintain cleanliness, queue in an orderly manner,
and use facilities responsibly. The refectory is not just a place to eat,
but a space to relax, recharge,
and connect with others before returning to academic activities.
